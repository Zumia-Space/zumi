# Contributor License Agreement (placeholder)

Outside contributions to Zumi need a signed Contributor License Agreement (CLA) before they can be merged.

Why: Zumi is planned to be dual licensed. When the app is released as open source, it will be published under AGPL-3.0 and may also be offered under other terms, so every contribution must be covered by an agreement that allows both.

The agreement text itself is not written yet. Until it is, please do not open pull requests with code changes; issues and bug reports are welcome. Questions: oss@zumia.space.
